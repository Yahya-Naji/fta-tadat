# Compliance-risk intelligence & research (summary)
_Illustrative — supports P2-3-1._

## Sources used
- Environmental scans from the strategic-planning cycle.
- Analysis of declarations and financial statements (VAT, CT, Excise).
- Audit results, including a small random-audit component to test compliance across samples.
- Third-party data: banks/financial institutions, Customs, emirate licensing.
- Exchange-of-information data from international partners.

## Studies
- Transfer pricing / profit shifting by large taxpayers with cross-border operations.
- Tax planning by high-wealth / high-income individuals.
- Hidden-economy activity (cash businesses, under-reporting).
- Behavioural / environmental factors affecting compliance (sector, size, demographics).

## Analytics maturity
- Mostly descriptive analytics and rule-based flags today. Predictive modeling / machine
  learning and bunching analysis are on the roadmap, not yet in routine use.
  _(This is the main reason P2-3-1 is assessed at B rather than A.)_
