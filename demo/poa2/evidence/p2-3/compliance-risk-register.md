# Compliance risk register (extract)
_Illustrative — supports P2-3-2._

Structured per IMF/OECD guidance; risks scored on a likelihood × consequence rating matrix
and linked to the annual business plan.

| Risk | Tax | Segment | Likelihood | Consequence | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CT large-segment non-filing | CT | Large | High | High | High |
| VAT refund fraud (duplicate/bogus) | VAT | Medium | Medium | High | High |
| Transfer pricing / profit shifting | CT | Large | Medium | High | High |
| Excise under-declaration | Excise | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Cash/hidden economy under-reporting | VAT/CT | Micro-Small | High | Medium | Medium |

## Coverage + quantification
- Covers all core taxes, the four obligations and key segments; sector ranking covers the
  largest sector, with broader sectors being added.
- Revenue-leakage estimated for VAT only so far (internal); not yet across all core taxes
  and not published. _(Why P2-3-2 is B not A — annual-planning linkage + partial sector/leakage coverage.)_
