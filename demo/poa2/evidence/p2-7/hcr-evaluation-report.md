# Human-capital-risk evaluation (extract)
_Illustrative — supports P2-7-2._

## What's in place
- The HR area conducts a formal evaluation of HCR status and the effectiveness of mitigation
  interventions, including a staff engagement survey.
- Findings are reviewed and acted on (training, retention, succession adjustments).

## Gaps (why this is C, not A/B)
- The evaluation is conducted **roughly every two years**, not annually.
- It is run **by the HR area itself**, not by a person independent of HR.
- The annual report covers HCRs **periodically**, not every year.

_Frequency (biennial) + lack of independence → P2-7-2 ≈ C, which (lowest-wins, M1) pulls the
overall POA 2 to C._
