# HR strategy + human-capital-risk register (extract)
_Illustrative — supports P2-7-1._

## HR strategy
- A comprehensive, current HR strategy is in place, owned by HR with the Strategy & Risk function.
- An open performance-management process operates across the organisation (objectives + reviews).

## HCR register (five areas)
| Area | Example risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Capability | Skills gap in data analytics / risk modeling | Training + targeted recruitment |
| Capacity | Succession for key leadership roles | Succession plans + mentoring |
| Compliance | Performance reviews not always objective | Standardised review process |
| Cost | Attrition of critical staff | Retention + workforce-cost analysis |
| Connection | Engagement / knowledge sharing | Engagement surveys + initiatives |

## Other
- Strategies address gender equality and balance.
- HR team trained in HCRs; managers made aware and supported.
- HCR register maintained with assessed/prioritised risks and mitigation owners.
