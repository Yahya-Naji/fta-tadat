# Compliance Improvement Plan 2025 (extract)
_Illustrative — supports P2-4._

## Scope
- Covers all core taxes (VAT, CT, Excise) and the four obligations (registration, filing, payment, accurate reporting).
- Addresses all risks rated **High** in the risk register.
- Large-taxpayer segment specifically covered; other segments partly covered.

## Structure (per risk)
- Risk → drivers → mitigation actions (mix of service, education, audit, enforcement) → owner → KPI → due date.
- Lesser risks are placed under **monitoring** rather than active intervention.

## Examples
- CT large-segment non-filing → targeted reminders + audit campaign (owner: Returns; KPI: on-time filing +10pts).
- VAT refund fraud → pre-refund risk screening (owner: Audit; KPI: fraud detected/prevented).

## Resourcing + status
- The plan is resourced and largely implemented; most actions on track this cycle.
- It is an **annual** plan aligned to the business-planning cycle (not multi-year).
