# Risk committee minutes + mitigation evaluation (extract)
_Illustrative — supports P2-5._

## Governance + cadence
- Compliance Risk Management Committee reviews mitigation progress **quarterly**.
- Minutes record: review of the prior-year plan, approval of the current plan, effectiveness
  of mitigation strategies, and decisions taken.

## Monitoring + evaluation
- Progress and impact are tracked against set objectives; status reported to senior management.
- The compliance impact (revenue collected + change in taxpayer behaviour) of the main
  activities over the past 1–2 years has been assessed in an evaluation report.

## Feedback loop
- Findings are documented and fed into the next compliance improvement plan.
- Policy makers were alerted to legal weaknesses exposing the system to risk (e.g. aggressive
  tax-planning gaps), with proposed remedies.

_Quarterly (not monthly) review + ~50–100% strategy coverage → P2-5 ≈ B._
