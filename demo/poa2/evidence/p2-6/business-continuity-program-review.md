# Business continuity program — review (extract)
_Illustrative — supports P2-6-2._

## Governance + monitoring
- The Operational Risk / Business Continuity Committee endorses the BC strategy and monitors
  implementation; corrective actions are tracked.
- The BC / disaster-recovery plan is reviewed and updated **annually**.

## Testing
- Disaster-simulation / tabletop exercises are conducted **annually**; results and
  recommendations are documented and acted on by senior management.
- Not all staff are involved in every exercise (rotated by function).

## Incidents
- When an incident occurs, a post-incident review is conducted and lessons fed back into the plan.

_Annual (not six-monthly) cadence + not-all-staff exercises → P2-6-2 ≈ B._
