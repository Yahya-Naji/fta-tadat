# Operational risk register + Business Impact Analysis (extract)
_Illustrative — supports P2-6-1._

## Process
- A structured process identifies, assesses and prioritises operational risks across the
  organisation, recorded in an operational risk register; part of the planning cycle.

## Operational risk register (sample)
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Owner | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EmaraTax outage > 4h | Medium | High | IT/Data | Redundancy + failover; monitoring |
| Cyber-security breach / data loss | Medium | High | Security | Controls, audit trail, MFA, SOC |
| Key-vendor (cloud) disruption | Low | High | IT/Data | Vendor SLAs + BC review |

## Business Impact Analysis
- BIA conducted with recovery time objective (RTO) and recovery point objective (RPO) per
  critical service; **refreshed about every two years** (not annually).
- A business-continuity strategy and documented recovery procedures with defined roles exist.
- Operational-risk training provided to staff; key suppliers' continuity capability reviewed.
- Formal BC governance with senior-management ownership.

_BIA every two years (not annual) → P2-6-1 ≈ B._
