# Risk governance — committee charters (extract)
_Illustrative._

## Compliance Risk Management Committee
- Purpose: approve and oversee the compliance improvement plan; review identified compliance risks and mitigation effectiveness.
- Chair: Director, Strategy & Risk. Members: heads of Audit, Returns, Registration, Taxpayer Services, Legal.
- Cadence: quarterly (with monthly progress dashboards).

## Operational Risk / Business Continuity Committee
- Purpose: endorse the business-continuity strategy; oversee operational + cyber risk and BC testing.
- Chair: Deputy DG (Support Services). Members: IT/Data, Security, Facilities, HR, Internal Audit (observer).
- Cadence: at least annually, plus after any major incident.

## Enterprise oversight
- The Senior Leadership Team reviews the enterprise risk register periodically under the FTA Enterprise Risk Management Policy.
