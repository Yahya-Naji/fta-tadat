BACKGROUND (not scored)
============================================================
Q1. Which unit sets risk policy / oversees it?
> The Strategy & Risk function sets risk-management policy under the FTA Enterprise
  Risk Management Policy; IT/Data owns operational/cyber risk and HR owns human-capital risk.
Q2. Active senior risk committees?
> Yes — a Compliance Risk Management Committee and an Operational Risk / Business
  Continuity Committee meet regularly; an SLT reviews enterprise risk.

============================================================
P2-3-1 — Intelligence gathering & research  (target B)
============================================================
> Builds compliance-risk knowledge across VAT, CT and Excise by segment and obligation.
> Environmental scans run in strategic planning; declarations + financial statements analysed.
> Audit results analysed, incl. a small random-audit component.
> Research into hidden economy + studies of transfer pricing/profit shifting, HWI, crypto.
> Environmental/behavioural factors considered in segmentation.
> Third-party data (banks, customs, licensing) used; analytics mostly basic, not advanced ML.

============================================================
P2-3-2 — Assess, rank, quantify  (target B)
============================================================
> Structured risk-assessment process consistent with IMF/OECD guidance.
> Covers all core taxes, key segments and the largest sector; broader sector ranking expanding.
> Linked to annual business planning (not a full multi-year strategic process).
> Maintains a compliance risk register describing each risk and its threat.
> Revenue-leakage estimates for some areas (VAT) only; not all core taxes; not published.

============================================================
P2-4 — Compliance improvement plan  (target B)
============================================================
> A CIP mitigates identified risks.
> Covers all core taxes, the four obligations and high risks; large-taxpayer segment specific.
> Lesser risks kept under monitoring.
> Annual plan, aligned to business planning.
> Resourced and largely implemented; most actions on track.

============================================================
P2-5 — Monitoring & evaluation  (target B)
============================================================
> Compliance Risk Management Committee oversees mitigation; reviews quarterly.
> Progress + impact monitored against objectives, reported to senior management.
> Compliance impact (revenue + behaviour) assessed over the past 1–2 years.
> Policy makers alerted to legal weaknesses.
> Findings documented and fed into the next plan.

============================================================
P2-6-1 — Operational risk identify/assess/mitigate  (target B)
============================================================
> Structured process for operational risks (IT failure, cyber, data loss) in planning.
> Operational risk register maintained.
> BIA with RTO/RPO conducted; refreshed about every two years.
> BC strategy + documented recovery procedures with defined roles.
> Operational-risk training provided; key suppliers' continuity reviewed.
> Formal BC governance with senior ownership.

============================================================
P2-6-2 — Business continuity program  (target B)
============================================================
> BC program tested; results/recommendations documented and acted on by senior management.
> BC/DR plan reviewed annually.
> Progress monitored and reported to senior management.
> Disaster-simulation exercises annually (not all staff each time).
> Post-incident reviews conducted when incidents occur.

============================================================
P2-7-1 — HCR capacity & structures  (target B)
============================================================
> HR (with Strategy & Risk) owns HR management; comprehensive, current HR strategy.
> Open performance-management process across the organisation.
> HCR register + formal process across the five areas (capability, capacity, compliance, cost, connection).
> Strategies address gender equality and balance.
> HR team trained in HCRs; managers aware and supported.

============================================================
P2-7-2 — HCR evaluation  (target C)
============================================================
> HCR status + mitigation impact evaluated by HR roughly every two years (not annually, not independent).
> Annual report covers HCR periodically rather than every year.
