# Core tax registration — legal basis (extract)
_Illustrative summary of registration provisions — not the verbatim law._

## VAT (Federal Decree-Law on Value Added Tax)
- **Mandatory registration**: taxable supplies/imports in the prior 12 months (or expected next 30 days) exceed **AED 375,000**.
- **Voluntary registration**: supplies/expenses exceed **AED 187,500**.
- Deregistration required when a person ceases to make taxable supplies or falls below the voluntary threshold.

## Corporate Tax (Federal Decree-Law on the Taxation of Corporations and Businesses)
- **All taxable persons** (resident juridical persons, and natural persons conducting business above the threshold) must register and obtain a Corporate Tax TRN, regardless of whether tax is ultimately due.
- Registration deadlines are set by FTA decision.

## Excise Tax (Federal Decree-Law on Excise Tax)
- Importers, producers and stockpilers of excise goods (tobacco, energy/carbonated/sweetened drinks, etc.) must register before carrying out activities.

## Who cannot / need not register
- Persons making only exempt supplies and wholly below thresholds.
- Government and qualifying entities follow specific provisions/exceptions.

## Identity & numbering
- Registration is tied to the **Emirates ID / establishment card**; the FTA issues a single **Tax Registration Number (TRN)**.
