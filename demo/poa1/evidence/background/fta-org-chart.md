# Federal Tax Authority — Registration function (org chart extract)
_Illustrative demo document._

Director General
└── Tax Operations Sector
    └── **Registration & Taxpayer Services** (owns the registration process + EmaraTax register)
        ├── Registration Unit — processes new registrations, amendments, deregistrations
        ├── Taxpayer Records & Data Quality — cleansing, duplicate/dormant management
        └── Taxpayer Services / Contact Centre — enquiries, portal support
    └── Compliance & Enforcement Sector
        └── Registration Compliance — detection of unregistered businesses
└── Support Services Sector
    └── IT & Data Department — EmaraTax platform, integrations, system audit trail
    └── Internal Audit — independent assurance (incl. registry accuracy reviews)

## Role notes
- **Registration & Taxpayer Services** is accountable for completeness and accuracy of the register.
- **IT & Data** maintains EmaraTax, integrations with ICP (Emirates ID) and licensing authorities, and the audit trail.
- **Registration Compliance** runs initiatives to find businesses required to register but not registered.
