# Unregistered-business detection plan (2024–2025)
_Illustrative operational plan._

## Objective
Identify businesses required to register (VAT / Corporate Tax) but not yet registered.

## Third-party sources used (systematic)
- **Customs** import data — importers above threshold not registered for VAT.
- **Emirate licensing** (DET / ADDED) and **free zones** — licensed entities with no TRN.
- **Economic-department** activity data.
- **E-commerce platform** analysis — online sellers / digital-service providers.
- **Labour / work-visa** data — sponsoring entities with no registration.

## Method
- Periodic data-matching of the above against the EmaraTax register; mismatches worked into
  a registration-outreach queue.
- When obligations broaden (e.g., Corporate Tax roll-out), the register is reviewed to ensure
  newly-liable taxpayers register for all applicable taxes.

## Not yet in place (roadmap)
- A formalised, intelligence-led **risk-based field-visit programme**.
- Use of **geolocalisation / remote-sensing imagery**.
_(Absence of these is why this indicator is assessed at band B rather than A.)_

## Monitoring
- Outcomes (businesses detected, registered) are recorded and reported to management
  (see `new-registrants-from-detection-2024.csv`).
