# EmaraTax — Tax Registration Form (field list)
_Illustrative reproduction of the captured fields._

## Applicant type
[ ] Natural person   [ ] Legal person (company / establishment)   [ ] Government entity

## Individual / natural person — captured fields
- Full name (EN + AR)
- Emirates ID number (identity anchor)
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Address (emirate, area, PO Box)
- Contact: mobile, email
- Appointed Tax Agent (name + TAN), if any
- Tax obligations selected: [ ] VAT  [ ] Corporate Tax  [ ] Excise
- Filing & payment frequency per selected tax

## Legal person / business — captured fields
- Legal name (EN + AR)
- Trade licence number + issuing authority (DET / ADDED / free zone)
- Date of incorporation / licence issue
- Business address + postal address
- Contact: phone, email, website
- ISIC / business activity classification
- Taxpayer segment (auto-derived: Micro / Small / Medium / Large)
- Related/associated parties & group members (name + TRN)
- **Beneficial owners / persons with significant control** (name + ID)
- Tax obligations + filing/payment frequency per tax

## On submission
- Identity validated against ICP (Emirates ID) and trade-licence register.
- Single high-integrity **TRN** issued (see numbering spec).
