# SOP — Identity verification at registration
_Illustrative standard operating procedure._

## Goal
Ensure applications are authentic and prevent bogus registrations (VAT & Corporate Tax are
refund/relief-fraud targets).

## Checks
- **All applicants**: Emirates ID / establishment-card validated electronically against ICP,
  and trade licence validated against the issuing authority, at the point of application.
- **Higher-risk profiles** (e.g., refund-likely sectors, new entities): supporting-document
  review completed **before** the TRN is activated.
- **Low-risk profiles**: a small share are verified **shortly after** registration
  (post-registration check) to keep onboarding fast.

## Notes
- Verification is mostly electronic; manual document review applies to flagged cases.
- This post-registration step for low-risk cases is the reason accuracy is assessed at
  band B rather than A.
