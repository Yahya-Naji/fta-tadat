# Internal Audit report — Registry accuracy (2024)
_Illustrative summary._

## Scope
Review of the accuracy and reliability of the EmaraTax taxpayer register across VAT,
Corporate Tax and Excise, covering FY2024.

## Opinion
**High level of confidence** in the accuracy of the active register, with a small number of
minor data-quality items being addressed.

## Key findings
- Duplicate rate within tolerance and trending down (see cleansing statistics).
- Identity verification operating effectively; controls evidenced for the sampled
  registrations.
- Cross-checking against external sources is effective but **not yet fully large-scale /
  automated** across all third-party databases — recommended for expansion.
- Dormant-account flagging working; a backlog of confirmations was cleared during the year.

## Recommendations
1. Extend automated third-party cross-checking to additional registers.
2. Move remaining low-risk identity checks to pre-registration where feasible.

## Status
Management accepted both recommendations; implementation in progress.
