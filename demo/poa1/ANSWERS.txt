============================================================
BACKGROUND (asked first — not scored)
============================================================

Q1. Under the tax laws, who must register / may register voluntarily / is not permitted?
> Registration is mandatory for any business exceeding the VAT threshold (AED 375,000
  taxable supplies), all taxable persons for Corporate Tax, importers/producers of excise
  goods, and employers for their obligations. Voluntary VAT registration is allowed above
  AED 187,500. Persons wholly below thresholds are not required to register; government
  entities follow specific rules.

Q2. What other government agencies are involved, and is registration info auto-shared?
> Business licensing sits with the emirate economic departments (Dubai DET, Abu Dhabi
  ADDED) and the free-zone authorities; identity is anchored on the Emirates ID (ICP).
  The FTA issues the TRN. Licensing and Emirates ID data are shared with the FTA through
  government data-exchange channels and validated at registration.

Q3. Which unit owns registration and the database?
> The Registration & Taxpayer Services function owns the process and the EmaraTax
  registration database, supported by the IT/Data department for system integrity.

============================================================
P1-1-1 — ADEQUACY OF INFORMATION + IT SUBSYSTEM  (target: A)
============================================================

Q1. For individuals, does the database hold the required fields?
> Yes — EmaraTax holds full name, address, contact details (phone/email of the taxpayer
  and any appointed tax agent), date of birth, gender, and the filing & payment obligations
  for each core tax the person is registered for, all keyed to the Emirates ID.

Q2. For businesses, does it hold the required fields?
> Yes — full legal name, business and postal address, phone/email/website, the TRNs of
  related/associated parties and group members, beneficial owners and persons with
  significant control, date of incorporation/licence, ISIC industry classification,
  taxpayer segment (e.g., Large Taxpayer), and filing & payment obligations per core tax.

Q3. Computerised or manual? Centralised or decentralised?
> Fully computerised on EmaraTax — a single, centralised national platform. There is no
  manual or decentralised register.

Q4. Numbering system — single unique high-integrity number? Check digit?
> Each taxpayer has one unique high-integrity TRN used across VAT, Corporate Tax and
  Excise. The TRN includes a self-validating check digit and is the single key across all
  core taxes.

Q5. Does the IT subsystem provide the 6 features (incl. MFA)?
> Yes — EmaraTax integrates with filing and payment processing, gives officers a
  whole-of-taxpayer view across all core taxes, supports deactivation of dormant accounts
  and deregistration with restorable archiving, generates registration management
  information, maintains a full audit trail of user access and changes, and offers secure
  online self-service registration/updates protected by multi-factor authentication.

============================================================
P1-1-2 — ACCURACY OF INFORMATION  (target: B)
============================================================

Q1. Documented procedures to remove inactive/duplicate/invalid — routine or ad hoc?
> Yes — documented procedures run on a regular schedule to flag dormant accounts and
  remove duplicate, inactive and invalid records.

Q2. Authentic applications + proof of identity before registration? How?
> Identity is verified against Emirates ID/ICP at registration for all applicants; for
  higher-risk profiles, document checks are completed before the TRN is activated, while a
  small share of low-risk cases are verified shortly AFTER registration.

Q3. Cross-checks against third parties? Large-scale automated? Third-party accuracy?
> Information is cross-checked against the licensing authorities and the Emirates ID
  register, and selectively against other sources. This is partly automated but not yet
  large-scale across every external database; third-party data is treated as reliable
  given the official government sources.

Q4. Reviewing repeat non-filers and updating the register?
> Accounts with no filing across consecutive periods are reviewed and re-classified
  (dormant/deregistered) where the taxpayer is found inactive.

Q5. Certainty on active counts? Internal/external audit in last 1-2 years?
> An internal audit of registry accuracy was completed in the last year and expressed a
  high level of confidence, with a few minor data-quality items being addressed.

============================================================
P1-2 — KNOWLEDGE OF THE POTENTIAL TAXPAYER BASE  (target: B)
============================================================

Q1. Third-party info to detect unregistered businesses?
> Yes — the FTA uses customs import data, emirate licensing records and economic-department
  data to identify businesses trading above the threshold that have not registered,
  including digital-service providers and online sellers.

Q2. Geolocalisation / remote-sensing imagery?
> Not currently used in a systematic way.

Q3. Review when the base broadens (e.g., threshold change)?
> Yes — when obligations expand (e.g., Corporate Tax roll-out), the register is reviewed to
  ensure newly-liable taxpayers register for all applicable taxes.

Q4. Risk-based unannounced visits + intelligence sources?
> Field visits to commercial districts occur but are not yet a formalised, intelligence-led
  programme.

Q5. Analyse online trading platforms?
> Some analysis of e-commerce platforms is done to spot unregistered sellers.

Q6. Past 1-2 years — outcomes monitored and reported?
> Yes — detection initiatives over the past year are documented, with the number of
  taxpayers added to the register reported to management.
