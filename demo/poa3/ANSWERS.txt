============================================================
BACKGROUND (not scored)
============================================================
Q1. Who owns taxpayer services / is there a strategy?
> The Taxpayer Services & Communications department owns information, education and
  contact-centre services, guided by a documented Taxpayer Services & Communication Strategy.
Q2. Through which channels are information and services delivered?
> Website, EmaraTax portal, contact centre (phone + email), in-person centres, social
  media and a mobile app — in Arabic and English.

============================================================
P3-8-1 — Scope of information  (target A)
============================================================
> Guides, FAQs and web pages cover all core taxes (VAT, Excise, CT) and the main
  obligations: how to register, file, pay, and what is taxable.
> Tailored to key segments (small business, large taxpayers, individuals); accessibility
  features for special needs.
> A documented services/communication strategy guides what is produced and for whom.

============================================================
P3-8-2 — Currency of information  (target B)
============================================================
> When laws/rates/procedures change, the policy team flags affected products and Taxpayer
  Services updates them — applied periodically (in batches), not instantly.
> Documented update procedure with a named owner; periodic check for out-of-date material;
  minor lags of a few weeks can occur after a change.

============================================================
P3-8-3 — Accessibility & ease of access  (target A)
============================================================
> Wide range of channels (website, portal, contact centre, in-person, email, social, app)
  in Arabic and English.
> Plain language, website search, mobile-optimised — taxpayers can usually self-serve
  without telephoning.

============================================================
P3-9-1 — Telephone enquiry waiting time  (target B — ~7 min)
============================================================
> Median (P50) waiting time to reach an agent over the last 12 months ≈ 7 minutes.
> Measured by the contact-centre platform and reported monthly; abandonment ≈ 9%.

============================================================
P3-9-2 — Written / electronic response time  (target B — ~92% in 21 days)
============================================================
> Published standard is 21 calendar days; ≈ 92% answered within it over the last 12 months.
> Turnaround tracked in the correspondence/case system and reported monthly.

============================================================
P3-10 — Reduce compliance costs  (target B)
============================================================
> Pre-filled fields/validations in EmaraTax, simplified record-keeping guidance for small
  business, fully online register/file/pay, and a tax-agent framework.
> Tax agents/intermediaries engaged via briefings + a dedicated channel for bulk filings.
> Impact measured only partially (online-filing uptake, processing time) — no full
  compliance-cost study yet.

============================================================
P3-11-1 — Use of feedback methods  (target A)
============================================================
> Range of methods used routinely: annual satisfaction surveys, focus groups, a tax-agent
  advisory panel, and complaint/contact-centre analytics.
> Obtained at least annually; covers key segments (individuals, SMEs, large taxpayers, agents).

============================================================
P3-11-2 — Acting on feedback  (target B)
============================================================
> Feedback analysed and fed into service-improvement plans; some changes (simplified portal
  screens, clearer VAT guidance) traceable to feedback.
> Specific improvements made over 1–2 years, though not every theme is closed-looped back
  to taxpayers.

============================================================
P3-12 — Initiatives to encourage accurate reporting  (target C)
============================================================
> Cooperative-compliance / enhanced-relationship arrangements with large taxpayers are being
  piloted, not yet an established programme.
> A private clarifications regime is in place and actively used; a public-ruling programme
  is still developing.
> Education has been one-off awareness campaigns (e.g. at Corporate Tax launch) rather than
  a sustained, targeted programme for higher-risk segments.
