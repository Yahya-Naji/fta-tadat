# Compliance-cost reduction initiatives (extract)
_Illustrative — supports P3-10._

## Initiatives in place
- **EmaraTax**: pre-filled fields and in-form validations reduce errors and rework.
- **Small business**: simplified record-keeping guidance and templates.
- **End-to-end online**: register, file and pay fully online (no paper).
- **Tax agents**: a registered tax-agent framework + a dedicated channel for bulk filings.

## Measurement
- Online-filing uptake and average processing time are tracked.

## Gap (why this is B, not A)
- Impact on taxpayers' compliance burden is measured **only partially** — there is no
  full compliance-cost / time-to-comply study yet.

_Several active initiatives + partial measurement → P3-10 ≈ B._
