# Initiatives to encourage accurate reporting (extract)
_Illustrative — supports P3-12._

## What's in place
- **Private clarifications regime**: taxpayers can request a binding clarification on how
  the law applies to their facts; the regime is in place and actively used.

## Still maturing (why this is C, not A/B)
- **Cooperative compliance** / enhanced-relationship arrangements with large taxpayers are
  being **piloted**, not yet an established programme.
- A **public-ruling** programme is still developing.
- Taxpayer education has been delivered as **one-off awareness campaigns** (e.g. at the
  Corporate Tax launch) rather than a sustained, targeted programme for higher-risk segments.

_Proactive engagement limited (clarifications only; pilots + one-off campaigns) → P3-12 ≈ C,
which (lowest-wins, M1) takes the overall POA 3 to C._
