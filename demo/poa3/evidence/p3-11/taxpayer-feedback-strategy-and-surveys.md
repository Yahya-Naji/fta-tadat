# Taxpayer feedback strategy + surveys (extract)
_Illustrative — supports P3-11-1 (use of feedback methods)._

## Methods used routinely
- Annual **taxpayer satisfaction / perception survey**.
- **Focus groups** with selected segments.
- A **tax-agent advisory panel** that meets periodically.
- **Analytics** from complaints and contact-centre contacts.

## Coverage
- Obtained at least annually; covers individuals, SMEs, large taxpayers and tax agents.

_Range of methods, routine, all key segments → P3-11-1 ≈ A._
