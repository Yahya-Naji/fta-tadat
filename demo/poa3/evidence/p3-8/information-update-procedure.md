# Information-update procedure (extract)
_Illustrative — supports P3-8-2 (currency)._

## Process
- Policy teams flag information products affected by a legislative, rate or procedural
  change; Taxpayer Services updates the products and republishes.
- A named owner (Content Lead) is accountable; a periodic review checks the catalogue
  for out-of-date material.

## Change log (sample)
| Product | Change trigger | Updated |
|---|---|---|
| VAT filing guide | Procedure update | within ~3 weeks |
| Corporate Tax guide | Law amendment | within ~4 weeks |

## Gap (why this is B, not A)
- Updates follow each change but are applied **periodically (in batches)** rather than
  immediately, so minor lags of a few weeks can occur.

_Updated for changes, but periodic not immediate → P3-8-2 ≈ B._
