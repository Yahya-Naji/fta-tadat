# Taxpayer Services & Communication Strategy (extract)
_Illustrative — supports POA 3 background + P3-8-1._

## Ownership
- The **Taxpayer Services & Communications** department owns taxpayer information,
  education and contact-centre services, reporting to the Director-General's office.
- A documented strategy sets objectives, target segments, channels and review cadence.

## Channels (Arabic + English)
- FTA website · EmaraTax online portal · contact centre (phone + email) · in-person
  service centres · social media · mobile app.

## Segments
- Individuals, small/medium businesses, large taxpayers, and tax agents — with tailored
  products and accessibility support for taxpayers with special needs.
